# Ethics, privacy and limitations
- Educational research prototype; not a medical device; no diagnosis, no medication advice.
- Demo or synthetic data only until institutional approval, valid consent and security review exist (see the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and confirm obligations with your institution).
- Consent is required to register a participant and can be withdrawn; deletion removes linked records.
- Least privilege: researchers and admins cannot read participant records; aggregates hide groups below the minimum size.
- Nutrition: no invented nutrient values or prices; costs are relative tiers; specialised diets are referred to professionals.
- Known gaps: SQLite not PostgreSQL, minimal token auth, no password reset, no real LLM chatbot, Hindi covers labels and headings but not long explanations.
